Our Story

Momo Mantra began with a single steamer basket and a family recipe from the hills of Darjeeling. Every morning we roll the dough by hand, fold each momo with eighteen pleats, and steam them in bamboo baskets — the way our grandmother taught us. No shortcuts, no freezers full of yesterday's dumplings.

We're proud to call Morrisville home, right in the heart of the Triangle. Come for the momos, stay for the chutney.
